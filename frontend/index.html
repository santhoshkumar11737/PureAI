Identified Pollution Patterns

Key insights generated from exploratory data analysis (EDA)

Traffic Hours Peak

PM2.5 and NO2 levels spike significantly during morning (7 AM - 9 AM) and evening (5 PM - 8 PM) commute hours due to vehicular exhaust.

Weekend Drop

Weekend pollution levels show a 15% to 25% reduction compared to weekdays as commercial transport and office commutes decrease.

Industrial PM10 Concentration

Industrial and manufacturing zones exhibit higher PM10 concentration throughout the day, relatively independent of traffic hours.

Night Atmospheric Inversion

Lower ambient temperatures during late night hours trap particulate matter closer to the ground, causing elevated AQI around 4 AM.

About the Machine Learning Project

The “Air Quality Pattern Analysis” project aims to analyze atmospheric pollution data, detect recurring air quality patterns across urban centers, and provide future prediction capabilities. By isolating key meteorological and traffic variables, machine learning algorithms can accurately forecast environmental risks and assist policymakers in taking proactive measures.
